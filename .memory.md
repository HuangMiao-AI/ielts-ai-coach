## Listening Vocabulary Lab

- The normal authenticated and guest Listening route now opens a session-only
  vocabulary and spelling trainer. The previous Mini Practice implementation,
  data, audio assets, and tests remain intact but are no longer reachable from
  the normal Listening route.
- The project-curated local bank contains 300 unique B1-C1 entries across 11
  IELTS-relevant categories. Each entry has a stable ID, English word, Chinese
  meaning, part of speech, level, and category; it is not presented as an
  official IELTS word list.
- Each learning round selects 10 unique words. The spelling challenge can reuse
  the most recently studied set, normalizes only surrounding whitespace and
  case, awards 10 points per exact answer, rejects duplicate submissions, and
  provides a summary, wrong-word review, and replay.
- Pronunciation uses the browser Web Speech API with en-GB, en-US, then other
  English voice preference. It handles asynchronously loaded voices, cancels
  prior speech on replay/word/page changes, and shows a clear fallback when
  speech or an English voice is unavailable.
- Guest and signed-in Listening progress is scoped to the current Streamlit
  session and never writes users, profiles, results, analytics, or Training
  Arena score. Stale or malformed session objects are discarded safely.
